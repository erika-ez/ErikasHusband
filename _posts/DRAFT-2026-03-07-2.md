Far away in the murky waters of the Pokemon world is Redgia - the rare red colored variant of the legendary Pokemon Lugia. They say that the red scale from a Redgia's tale is a provides peace to a mind that cannot regulate itself. Just a glimpse of the Redgia's scales is sought out by only the most daring of adventurers. So there I was, Nintendo 3DS in hand, standing right in front of Lugia. I would press the A button, commencing the encounter. The figure of Lugia would appear in the screen first obscured, then revealed with a growl like "grwahll." If the lugia didn't sparkle and wasn't red, I reset the game console with a button combination: L + R + Select, L + R + Select, L + R + Select. The process would begin all over again: encounter Lugia, Lugia doesn't sparkle and isn't red, reset the 3DS. 

With each button combination, the 3DS would reset. I wanted to find the Redgia, I had to find Redgia, the unconquererble would be conquered, I knew it deep in my heart that it would happen. But how long would it take?

is this a practice of healing? why am i drawn to the rigidness of repititive actions? picking the skin around my nails, eating the same food every day, wearing the same colors and comforting sweaters. routine is regulating.
