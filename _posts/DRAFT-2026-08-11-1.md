subjects: my house as the spot to hang, Cobra

I've had to make a change in my habits, but it's been worthwhile. My apartment is becoming the spot for hanging out. I've been hosting band practices and whatnot, because I have a big living room with high ceilings, and I'm always volunteering my place as the meet up spot. I'm learning to clean up as I go, wipe down counters, dishes in the dishwasher at the end of the day, vacuum up crumbs when I see them, put away my laundry - that sort of thing. The point is to maintain a consistently clean space. I can't live like a messy teenager anymore! It's not good for my brain. And the process is a comfort, as if clearing clutter in the physical clears clutter in my mind. I want a space that, if someone were to knock on my door and ask for an impromptu hang, I could invite them in without feeling panicked about the state of my room. Or do you think that it's a glimpse of my childhood flashing in the rear? Even now, when I visit home, it's a bloody mess (,,, nobody knows how to clean ,,,)
