I always feel guilty when I check out too many books from the library. I know I shouldn't feel bad for merely using the library for it's purpose, but I never read them all, and I get this nagging feeling that there's somebody desperatly waiting for whatever book(s) I have, and they're checking the library website everyday, and they're seeing the book is checked out, and they're like, _Damn it, it has to be Erika, Erika checked out the book and I hate her, she will fall by my hand and my hand alone_, and they might be someone adept in the sword, and they'll take their sword and find me, cooped up in my apartment, books on the floor, and they'll challenge me to a dual, which of course I'd win, knowing that I am also adept in the sword, but it would all be so sad, an unneccessary tragedy, because no lost library book should result in violence. Still, none of this stopped me from checking out a pile of books when I meant to only pick up one, _Arrangements in blue : notes on loving and living alone,_ which to my knowledge is about a single woman living (coping? understanding?) life as a single woman. I wouldn't typically read such a book as it sounds sappy, but I saw it mentioned in a review of bell hooks' novel _Communion: The Female Search for Love_ and I did like that book (besides the very long chapter on women "choosing" to be lesbian), so I decided to give it a looksee.
