Did I travel to St. Louis for a reset? Did I travel to St. Louis to go back in time - to a simpler time, to walks around campus, to drunken escapades, to chips for breakfast-lunch-dinner, to sleeping in until 1:00PM, to walking into lecture halls late (or not at all), to childish endeavers, to the defining moments of college life (at least, my college life) - the containerized chaos of it all?

Whatever the reason, the number of oddities I encountered in St. Louis stood out to me. Was it this weird back in college? Maybe being isolated in the university bubble distracted me from the weird. Not that the weird dampered my trip - t I'd say it enhanced it, because I have more to write about. I arrived Friday afternoon and spent the whole day hanging out with Grace and Kobe. We got lunch together at City Park Grill in Tower Grove. Being a fry connoisseur, I got an order of fries (which they doubled just for me). I'd rate them a 7/10. Good size and shape, good amount of salt, and fresh tasting, but I had to dig to get the nice crispy fries that taste the best. 

Then we went to Found Vintage in South Grand. I've been to Found before, I don't remember visiting this location but definetly the one on the Delmar Loop and the larger Found by the Pound in Berkeley. While I was shopping, Erin, my suitemate from freshman year texted me "Are you in St. Louis?" out of the blue. My head snapped left and right - was she behind me? Was she in the walls? I didn't ask but just responded "yeah!" and we made a plan to meet later that night. Found had colorful clothes with lots of patterns that were moderatly overpriced, but as it usually goes, I found a dress I liked, gray with roses (two things I love...) and bought it. I didn't like that nobody said hello when we walked in and the cashier was weirdly silent when I checked out. He wasn't Gen Z, but he did that "Gen Z stare" people talk about. I suppose that was one of the oddities I encountered, but not _too_ odd.

Later we visited Circa Now! records. The store felt like a time capsule. They were playing this band called _Meat Puppies_ and had a CRT TV playing a music video. I'd like one of those for my room, I could put it in the corner and play weird videos and make it look artsy. Or maybe just have the static playing - hypnosis? The man at the front said nothing as we browsed through boxes of records. There were lots of vinyls, video tapes, CDs, and the like, but I wasn't in the mood for spending more money, although Kobe got a record for herself. 

Later we went to Up-Down Arcade Bar, which Kobe has been hyping up for a while, specifically for their Friday special. I couldn't remember if I been there before but when I walked in I realized I had (like 4 years ago?), but I never had the Friday special, a huge slice of pizza for $2. I was happy! Although I would rate the pizza a 6.5 - which Kobe did not like. It's wasn't bad, but it's not _exceptional._ Like for $2, it's great, but for the regular price of $5 - I would pass. Am I hating??

5 strange things i encountered in the midwest:
• The shitting turtle: I saw a turtle in forest park. It was sitting in what looked like a pile of mud. Then I saw it slowly stretch and strain its body until the mushy excrement pushed out. According to google, turtle poop should be solid not mushy, so this turtle was probably going through some diarrhea, perhaps due to low fiber or a parasite.
• Art Museum Man: As i wandered through the art museum with my friends, a man raced passed us. I heard him shout something like "beautiful women everywhere..." I assumed he was on the phone. He came back around and asked if we were over 18. I lied and said no and he left.
• Naked Man by the Pillar: We were in tower grove I think. My friend had parked her car on the street. Down the road I saw a building with white pillars in front, and a man posing, completely naked, as if he was a renaissance statue. Art or mental illness?
• Ant Farm: My sister remarked that she saw a bunch of ants by the garage door and she was "triggered." I didn't think much of it, ants live outside and I figured it couldn't be that many. When I went outside there was a huge pile of them and they blended in with each other. It almost looked like a pile of dirt, but moving.
• Young Boy on Bicycle: My friends and I were walking past the WashU apartments on the loop. A little boy on a bike zoomed past, screaming in a gremlin like voice: "I'm gonna hit you!" The kids are alright!
