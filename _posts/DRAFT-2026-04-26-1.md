title: side quests/ learning to open up my heart

had swimming lessons with alexandria. she is so patient i was a mess. embarrasingly, i thought i could swim without getting my hair/face wet. lol. i tried to float. she said i had to trust her, and i do, i just couldnt relax my body enough to become a floating mass. it was like i couldnt let go. i didnt want to get water in my eyes. i couldnt be vulnerable in that moment, embrace the embarrasment, i couldnt open my heart

whenever joyce goes out, she picks a new persona to embody, a new name, a new ethnicity. i wonder if i should start doing that, if that would be a way to open my heart

band practice. i really enjoy it. sometimes i am able to open my heart. i wrote some lyrics i did share it. which was opening my heart. so painfully embarrasing but now that i've gotten over that i can only write better stuff. although the embarassment of that vulnerable moment did seem to wear down on me. the entirety of practice feels like a vulnerable moment for me. im not great at bass, i have to sit in front of these people and suck. and we want to learn so many songs and raul and phuong can learn it so fast. i feel like a bottle neck a tank. and these dumb thoughts wear down on me and eventually im like. i dont want to play anymore. like a kid. but i have to embrace the suck. im learning to open my heart, i just have to trust in everyone around me that they'll hold me up. that they wont let me sink and laugh. im learning. but i had a fun day. ive made too many plans for the week.

i want to pursue many side quests i've realized. phuong said that. even my sister was surprised im in a band. how to balance it all? everybody says, you only need an hour a day to get good at anything. but i want to write poems, i want to blog, i want to learn the igbo language, i want to paly bass. i want to read, i want to play jrpgs, i want to write fiction. im trying to go to grad school, thats a whole other thing. i want to be fit, i want abs, i want to ride my exercise bike, i want to go to the gym, i want to hang out with my friends, i want to make new friends. i want to find a husband??? is there enough hours to do this, and go to work, and have an hour to sit and stare into space because i am an introvert. it was silly, we were going out to get food and i was in such a rush to put on my docs, they take forever to put on. and lexi is like, its ok, take your time. and im like wow, holy shit, im always in a rush. like my mind is like, go go go, because im trying to fit in all these side quests, and thats why im a bit overwhelmed. i dont know how i will balance it all, i'll just have to find a way. i'll just drink more blooms.
