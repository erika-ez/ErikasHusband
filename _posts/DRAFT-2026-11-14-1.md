I got my Flu and Covid-19 vaccine today at CVS. It was a breeze, although my arm was sore for 2 days. Every time I'm at this CVS I pass by the Halloween clearance, which at this point is weeks old, and I contemplate buying a bag of the sour patch kids or the Halloween themed goldfish. I tell myself I don't need it - candy is bad for my teeth, there's goldfish at the office, etc etc. But this time, I folded and purchased a bag of Sour Patch Kids that glow in the dark. I finished the bag after work, then looked at the nutrition facts afterwards. I don't know how it is possible for a food to have that many calories and zero grams of protein, but they did it! 

That night, we (V, Maya, Lanae, Joyce, and I) went to Sugarcane lounge for _Down in the Basement,_ a monthly (?) gathering for Black folks in the city to mix and mingle. I ordered a side of fries with a buffalo and habanero sauce, which were very good (V said, it's the best food she's had in the valley thus far.) Although I liked the games they provided, the music in the lounge was ridiculously loud, to the point that it seemed misleading to call it a social event, because who can socialize in this environment when nobody can hear? Still, I don't regret going, even if I can't say I talked to anyone new. Firstly, because hanging out with my friends is always worthwile, and second, because I sense that I am becoming a homebody in a not good way - like I'm so afraid of "wasting" time, or breaking my routine, that I don't go anywhere, when really, it's not like I would be doing anything important at this hour, and there's a whole weekend ahead of me that I can spend doing whatever I like.
