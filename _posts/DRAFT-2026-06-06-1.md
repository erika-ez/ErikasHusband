title: obsession thoughts

this might be a niche example, but have you ever seen those youtube videos, and they're titled something like "MANIFEST YOUR SPECIFIC PERSON ✨ FAST ✨ ✨ ACTUALLY WORKS ✨ MEDITATION ✨ ANCIENT FREQUENCIES ✨ 333 ✨ 222 ✨ ?" The comments will be filled with desperate people - "i hope this works" and success stories (or internet fibbers) - "this worked!" since maturing past the age of 14, I've always found these sort of things strange. trying to force someone in your life through magic tricks isn't right - you're trying to remove their automony through youtube videos. if it worked, would the result truly be what you want? someone who is meant to be in your life will simply be in it... no subliminals required.

this is the basis of the movie Obsession, in which "protagonist" (if you call him that) Baron "Bear" Bailey makes a wish with a strange toy for his crush to love him "more than anything in the world." it goes as well as you would expect.

Bear is the type of man that I dislike (or even hate). incredibly weak, unable to take hold of his own life and make decisions, incel-adjacent, and ultimately, extremely predatary. after he makes his wish, he sees Niki's behavior make a 180 change. His attempts to express concern - to Niki, to Niki's friends, to himself - are just ways to assauge his guilt, because he never stops having sex with her (until she smashes someone's skull with a brick).
