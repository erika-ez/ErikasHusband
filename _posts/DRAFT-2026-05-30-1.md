Desire, my prison in which I've built brick by brick through observations of men and women. 

There is nothing more terrifying than the look of desire in a man's eyes. And yet, it's alluring. the thrill of the chase. 

although the word chasing is doing a lot. i keep the feeling of wanting internally and use it as fuel to stay in shape. 

i want to freeze my eggs and give birth in signapore when im 35.

there was a time that desire was an unknown concept. i couldn't even name the body parts of a boy. then i saw my first crush and desire became my new rubiks cube, twisting and turning into different directions - how can i contort myself into venus?

this is not to say i dislike experiencing desire, i enjoy it very much, it makes me feel human. i just long for the days where none of this mattered - not just desire, everything, being a cog in the machine, spinning and spinning.
