title: There's a stranger in my car. Terraplananananana

Terraplana. Put it in your calendar, Raul says. Ok, well, it's in my calendar. Terraplana concert ok cool. I did need to go to a concert, it's been a while. And I was feeling creatively bankrupt as I like to put it. I talked about it with Kobe. She says (paraphrasing): this is a pivotal moment. A moment for growth. I choose to believe.

Dear reader, I can't say I remember this concert in it's entirety. What can I recall... the venue was a small coffee shop, no, "kava bar" in Tempe. There was a corner with couches and board games. There were string lights above the tiny stage. A T.V. playing some animated abstract image. What's kava? The waitress says it gets you high but not actually high. Kind of like ketamine? I don't trust any drugs that start with k, so I declined to purchase. Raul wanted a beer, but they only had kava drinks. Too bad!

A band called [Lovre](https://www.instagram.com/lovrephx/) went on. They didn't have a bassist but they still sounded good. The lead vocalist had a nice voice. I shook his hand after the show and he complimented my earrings, and so for 5 seconds I imagined our lives together, but I didn't think our children would look appealing, so I dismissed the thought. Raul chatted with them more and discovered that they were on the search for a bassist. "She plays bass," he said, and I was like, dawg, I can't help here. I am a baby bassist. But good luck to them.

Terraplana went on, and what do I like about [Terraplana](https://open.spotify.com/artist/0mOiRg1cqDkCESsFozsuZU?si=ZFKPQPJRSr2N-uOQPJWJtg)? They're from Brazil, and their music is in Portuguese. I like that I can't understand the words, only the feeling. They do have the core shoegaze traits, distorted guitars and vocals, slowed melodies, but there's still this clarity there, it's not completely distorted, you can still here the music, if that makes sense. It's not completely sleepy, like [Lovesliescrushing](https://open.spotify.com/artist/06yvjjrPokJGC66DzFfCkF?si=bB2V7R36Q2eOqrzWxTsX3A) for example (and I do like Loveliescrushing, Dear Reader. I'm just trying to help you understand). In other words, I enjoyed the show, it was engaging, and they were very happy to be there, as they conveyed eagerly. Although, as I usually do, I had that moment during a concert where I disassociate and contemplate my 5-year-plan. 5-year-plan... what's my 5-year-plan? I've always thought, I'll get my Computer Science Master's. Use that to make more money. Self-fund (or not!) my MFA. Publish book. Somewhere in between, have an original song published? Go to Japan? Should I aim bigger? Move to the bay area. Join an AI startup. Profit. Aim bigger? Go to to moon? Live there, have children there, little moon children with moon skulls and moon brains? Aim bigger? Become the sun?
