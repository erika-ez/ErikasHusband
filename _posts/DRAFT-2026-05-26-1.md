title: white shirts

Just once I'd like to keep a white shirt clean. Every white shirt in my closet hangs blemished by my clumsiness - spilled almond oil, blots of sweat, drops of food, all if it seeps into the seems, it stays stuck in the fabric like a memory. And the white pants, I can't forget the 2 pairs I white pants I own and never wear. Honestly, I'm afraid my period will surprise my and stain them crimson. Yes this has happened before, until a nice girl tapped me on the shoulder. What I didn't know: my body was not simply bleeding, but building concepts of desire. It was the beginning of the cycle: feel _something?_, pull my hair, bleed me dry. There was a time I couldn't distinguish boy from girl. Now I ask: how can I contort myself into Venus?

Lately desire has spilled from me like an overflowing cup. Not like a waterfall, but a gentle stream. It's only natural, merely my body's attempt to pack up boredom with dopamine. I'm experiencing the full range of a young woman's emotions: desire, envy, rage, jealousy, and in the end, joy. I don't know what this has to do with white shirts. Maybe that each stain represents an action - something I have done causes an immovable change. White shirts, are they manufacturered to be canvases? Or, was there a way to avoid these stains? If I had moved in the right path, if I had made the _correct_ choice(s), if if if, see how easy it is to fit the entirety of the human experience into if statements? 

Something I discussed with Kobe yesterday: negative feedback loops.
