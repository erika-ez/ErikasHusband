title: white shirts

Just once I'd like to keep a white shirt clean. Every white shirt in my closet hangs blemished by my clumsiness - spilled almond oil, blots of sweat, drops of food, all if it seeps into the seems, it stays stuck in the fabric like a memory. Lately desire has spilled from me like an overflowing up. I'm experiencing the full range of a young woman's emotions: desire, envy, rage, jealousy, and in the end, joy. I don't know what this has to do with white shirts. Maybe that each stain represents an action - something I have done causes an immovable change. White shirts, are they manufacturered to be canvases? its like they are produced to change. and what of me. there's a girl i knew with skin like glass, smooth as a pearl. meanwhile my skin changes with the shifts in the wind. meanwhile everything around me changes in indiscernable movements, butterfly effecting into something unrecognizable. im being told that ive changed but i dont see it. its obvious to everyone else though
