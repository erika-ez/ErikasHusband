title: white shirts

whats the point of white shirts. they never stay clean, at least in my possession. mostly stains from skincare, almond oil, the occasional sauce. its like they are produced to change. and what of me. there's a girl i knew with skin like glass, smooth as a pearl. meanwhile my skin changes with the shifts in the wind. meanwhile everything around me changes in indiscernable movements, butterfly effecting into something unrecognizable. im being told that ive changed but i dont see it. its obvious to everyone else though
