title: musical impulse as of june 21 2026

I recently read Hua Hsu's novel _Stay True._ In the novel, Hsu explains that he ran a zine and often published reviews of the music he was currently into. Being that this blog functions of my own sort of zine, and I do often include music I am listening to into my posts, why not dedicate an entire post to it? Maybe it will be a series, as my rotation shifts often. I've read a lot of music publications and I've always admired how they can describe music. I often struggle to describe [the sound of music](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6f0T6UV-HiI) beyond "I like this song," "it's a happy/sad/cheery/upbeat song." I have to consiously ask myself, if a deaf person were to read my words, would it capture the essence of the music? I suppose (as Kobe would say) this is my writing prompt.
