title: Trois Chaises (Triptych, 2026)

(commit crimes lane - Joyce)

In my apartment is three chairs (actually 4, if you count my desk chair. Or 6, if you count the chairs at the kitchen table. or 8, if you count the barstools. Or 9, if you count the couch. Or 10, if you count the toilet. Or-). Two of these chairs are new additions, as earlier in the year [I had a conversation with my sister that made me realize I need a lounge chair, somewhere to relax.](https://erikashusband.blog/2026/01/10/4/). It has been a journey, but I'm happy to announce that I've gotten a new chair!

1. The first chair.

This chair I got early in my move to Arizona. I started playing the bass guitar, but I noticed if I wanted to sit down, I couldn't sit at my office chair to play bass because it has arm rests, and the bed was awkward because I would sink down. There was the barstool chairs in the kitchen, but I didn't want to go back and forth moving the chair to my room and back. So I went of Facebook marketplace and found an office chair without arm rests and picked it up. It was lightweight so I easily moved it up the stairs and it was adequete for it's purpose. Sometimes, when I didn't want to be by a screen, I'd sit on that chair and read or write or think. I started to realize there was an element of relaxation missing. I'd sit on the chair and because it was so rigid and upright, I felt like I had to be productive. And thus, it appeared I'd outgrown the usefulness of this chair, and I embarked on a quest to find a new one.

2. The second chair.

I will start by saying I will not disclose the amount I paid for this chair. It's not good. It's really not. Anyway, I got this one off of Facebook marketplace. I had saved a bunch of chairs and was going back and forth about it. I asked the seller if it would fit in a Camry and he said "yes, def," so I decided fuck it, why not? It had only been a week after I got my wisdom teeth out and I think the anesthesia hadn't worn off completely, which in turn impaired my judgement. So I drove 20 minutes south and the seller sold me the chair and it looked alright. I tried to negotiate, and I was wearing the ice pack around my head hoping it would evoke sympathy which in turn would evoke a lower price, but he barely budged on price. In the end I shook his hand and we had a deal. I put the chair in my car and headed home, and noticed a gross smell filling up the vehicle - marijuana. This is no judgement to marijuana users but I don't want my car to smell like that and now it smells. Like. That. But no need to worry, I can fix it! I get home and put the chair on the balcony and scrub it down with detergent and oxi-clean and spray it with fabric spray. Then I wait.
