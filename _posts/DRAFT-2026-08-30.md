title: write about the rain

what can i say about rain. i don't like it
i don't like how it sticks to my hair
and blurs my glasses
snow is pretty, snow moves slowly through the air enough that i cannot be overwhelmed by the pitter patter and the splashes
and the hail while little missles is at least an interesting phenomenon
rain ruins the day 
i hate the feeling of wet mud under my shoes
oh and how it stains my shoes! my pants! disgusting

its been raining terribly in illinois. once my dad took my brothers car for a spin, got caught in a flood. the car stopped on the road post-flood damage. it was left there on the road, and the sheriff came to the house and demanding to speak to my confused and terrified brother
look at the chaos the rain causes
